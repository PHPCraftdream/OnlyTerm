## Contributing

Thanks for considering donating your time and energy!  Any contribution is
valued, even if it is just to highlight a typo.

OnlyTerm is a fork of [WezTerm](https://github.com/wezterm/wezterm). For
anything specific to this fork, use this repository's
[issue tracker](https://github.com/PHPCraftdream/OnlyTerm/issues).

* [Building from Source](install/source.md)

You needn't be a crack Rust coder; if you spot a typo or feel like you can see
a way to improve these docs, your contribution is welcome!
