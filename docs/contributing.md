## Contributing

Thanks for considering donating your time and energy!  Any contribution is
valued, even if it is just to highlight a typo.

OnlyTerm is a fork of [OnlyTerm](https://github.com/wezterm/wezterm); for
anything specific to this fork, please use this repository's own issue
tracker. The following upstream resources may also be helpful:

* [Building from Source](install/source.md)
* [Where to find things and hacking on the codebase](https://github.com/wezterm/wezterm/blob/master/CONTRIBUTING.md#contributing-to-onlyterm)
* [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/wezterm/wezterm/discussions)
* [Realtime conversation on Element.io/Gitter](help.md)

You needn't be a crack Rust coder; if you spot a typo or feel like you can see
a way to improve these docs, your contribution is welcome!
